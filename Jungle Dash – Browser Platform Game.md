# **Jungle Dash – Browser Platform Game**

**Goal (What do you want to build?)**

I want to build a fun **2D platform adventure game** using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The player controls a character who runs, jumps over obstacles, collects coins, defeats enemies, and tries to reach the end of each level.

The game will be inspired by classic platform games like Mario, but it will have its **own characters, levels, enemies, and style**.

## **What will it look like?**

* A colorful side-scrolling game world  
* A character that can run left and right  
* Platforms the player can jump on  
* Coins and gems to collect  
* Enemies moving around the level  
* Spikes, holes, and other obstacles  
* A health or lives counter  
* A score counter  
* A timer  
* A finish flag or treasure chest at the end of each level

## **What type of game is it?**

It is a **2D platform adventure game**.

The player uses the keyboard to move:

* **Left Arrow / A** – Move left  
* **Right Arrow / D** – Move right  
* **Space / Up Arrow** – Jump

The player must avoid enemies and obstacles while collecting as many coins as possible.

## **What problem does it solve / why might people use it?**

* It gives players a fun game they can play directly in their browser.  
* Players can challenge themselves to complete levels faster.  
* Coins, enemies, power-ups, and different levels make the game replayable.  
* Players can compete with friends for the highest score.  
* It combines creativity, coding, animation, and game design.

## **Describe from the point of view of somebody using your website. What would it be like?**

You open the website and click **Play Game**.

You choose your character and the first level begins.

Your character starts on the left side of the screen. You run through the level, jump onto platforms, collect coins, and avoid enemies.

You might jump on an enemy to defeat it or collect a special power-up that gives you an ability.

As you move forward, the level becomes harder with moving platforms, bigger jumps, spikes, and stronger enemies.

When you reach the treasure chest at the end, you complete the level and see your score.

You can then continue to the next level or replay the level to try to get a better score.

# **Features I Plan to Add**

### **Different Levels**

* **Level 1 – Green Jungle**  
* **Level 2 – Desert Adventure**  
* **Level 3 – Ice Mountain**  
* **Level 4 – Volcano**  
* **Level 5 – Space World**

Each level will become more difficult.

### **Coins and Gems**

Players can collect:

* Coins  
* Gems  
* Stars  
* Treasure chests

Collecting them increases your score.

### **Enemies**

Different enemies could include:

* Slimes  
* Angry bugs  
* Robots  
* Flying creatures  
* Boss enemies

### **Power-Ups**

Players can find special power-ups such as:

* **Speed Boost** – Run faster  
* **Super Jump** – Jump higher  
* **Shield** – Protect yourself from one hit  
* **Magnet** – Pull nearby coins toward you  
* **Double Coins** – Earn twice as many coins  
* **Extra Life** – Get another chance

### **Character Shop**

Players can use coins to unlock different characters.

For example:

* Ninja  
* Robot  
* Knight  
* Alien  
* Dinosaur

### **Different Game Modes**

**Adventure Mode**  
Complete levels one at a time.

**Time Attack**  
Finish the level as fast as possible.

**Coin Rush**  
Collect as many coins as possible before time runs out.

**Endless Mode**  
The level keeps going until the player loses.

### **Boss Battles**

At the end of some worlds, the player has to defeat a large boss.

Bosses could have different attacks and require the player to jump, dodge, and attack at the correct time.

### **Daily Challenge**

Every day, players could receive a special challenge such as:

* Collect 100 coins  
* Complete a level without losing health  
* Defeat 20 enemies  
* Finish a level in under 60 seconds

### **Missions**

Players could complete missions to earn rewards.

Examples:

* Collect 50 coins  
* Jump 100 times  
* Defeat 10 enemies  
* Finish 3 levels  
* Find a hidden treasure chest

### **Leaderboard**

Players could compete for:

* Highest score  
* Fastest level completion  
* Most coins collected  
* Most levels completed

### **Multiplayer**

Later, I could add multiplayer where two players compete on the same level.

They could race to see who reaches the finish first.

### **Friends**

Players could:

* Add friends  
* Challenge friends  
* Compare scores  
* Race against each other

### **Sound and Music**

The game could have:

* Jump sounds  
* Coin sounds  
* Enemy sounds  
* Power-up sounds  
* Victory music  
* Background music
